Cheat sheet

JWT, the safe way

🔏Always verify the signature — and pin the algorithm
⏱️Keep access tokens short-lived; refresh them
🙈Payload is readable — no passwords or secrets
🔒HTTPS only; prefer httpOnly, Secure cookies
